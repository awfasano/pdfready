PDFReady PRIVACY

Your files.
Your device.

PDFReady processes the files you choose on your device. The app has no developer-run processing server, accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK.

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Applies to PDFReady for iPhone and iPad, the dedicated Mac version in preparation, and this product website.

What stays in the app

PDFReady stores working copies of imported PDFs, selected photos and scans, with page order, selection and export settings, in its private local workspace. The workspace is retained across launches. Working source copies are removed when no pages refer to them, or when you clear the workspace. Temporary export PDFs are cleared on the next launch. Originals are not altered. Save an output through the system destinations you choose if you need to keep it; the prepared Mac version offers Save PDF Copy. Each workspace stays on its own device. PDFReady does not automatically synchronize editable workspaces between Mac and iPhone.

Permissions and your providers

Camera permission is requested to scan paper only on supported iPhone or iPad hardware. The prepared Mac version does not offer camera scanning. The system Photos picker provides the photos you select rather than broad library access. Files access is limited to the documents you choose. Scans and working copies stay in app storage unless you choose to share an output.

Photos or Files may download an item from your own iCloud or other cloud provider. Those providers handle that transfer under their own policies. PDFReady does not upload your working library to a developer service. Device backup may include app data depending on your system settings.

When you choose to share

System sharing sends the finished file to the app, person or destination you select. In the prepared Mac version, Save PDF Copy writes a copy to the folder you choose. Saving to your own cloud drive or sharing to another device is handled by that destination; it transfers the PDF, not the editable workspace. That recipient receives the file and may process or retain it under its own policy. Nothing is sent to our support inbox automatically.

Support email

If you email awfasano@gmail.com, we receive the address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive files unless needed and deliberately shared. Ask by email if you would like a support conversation deleted; some records may need to be retained for applicable obligations.

This website

This static website does not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, signup forms or remote fonts. Its hosting provider may receive normal request information such as IP address and browser details to deliver and secure the pages. Email links open your mail app; they do not submit a form to this website.

Removing your working data

Remove pages or clear the workspace to remove working copies. Use the in-app clear-workspace control to remove local working copies before removing the app. Copies previously saved or shared elsewhere remain at those destinations. Original source files are not deleted by clearing the app’s workflow.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if these data practices change. For a privacy question, contact awfasano@gmail.com and name PDFReady in the subject.
