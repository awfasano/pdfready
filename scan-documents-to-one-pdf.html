Scan documents into one PDF

Scan paper pages, add photos from your library, mix in existing PDFs, and finish with a single PDF you can preview and share.

Free to download. Your first 3 PDFs are free. Pro is $4.99 once, or a 7-day free trial then $2.99 per year.

How to do it in PDFReady

  1. Open PDFReady and tap Scan to use the document camera on a supported iPhone or iPad. Allow camera access if asked.
  2. Scan each page, or import selected photos or existing PDFs instead.
  3. Choose the pages to keep and arrange them in order.
  4. Name the new PDF, choose a mode and size limit if you need one, then create it.
  5. Check the measured size, preview every page, and save to Files or share it.

What you can do

  • Scan paper with the document camera on supported iPhone or iPad hardware.
  • Import photos from your library.
  • Combine scans, photos and PDFs in one packet.
  • Set a size limit if the recipient has one.

Questions

Does PDFReady make scans searchable?

No. This version does not add OCR or searchable text. Scans stay as page images.

Why can’t I scan on my device?

Scanning needs supported iPhone or iPad hardware and camera permission. If it is denied, use Open Settings to review it. You can still add PDFs or photos.

Can I scan on a Mac?

Camera scanning is not offered in the Mac version. Import PDFs or selected photos instead.

Where does my scan go?

Working copies stay on your device. Export files are temporary, so save the finished PDF to Files or share it to keep it.
