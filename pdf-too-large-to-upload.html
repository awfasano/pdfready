PDF too large to upload?

Portals and forms often reject PDFs over a size limit. PDFReady lets you pick only the pages you need, choose a size limit, and see the measured size before you upload.

Free to download. Your first 3 PDFs are free. Pro is $4.99 once, or a 7-day free trial then $2.99 per year.

How to do it in PDFReady

  1. Open PDFReady and add the PDF from Files (up to 100 MB per PDF).
  2. Select only the pages the upload needs, using a range such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.
  3. Choose Balanced or Smaller, then pick a size limit: 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 MB, or enter a custom limit from 0.05 to 100 MB.
  4. Create the PDF and read the measured size. If it is still over the limit, select fewer pages or choose Smaller and create it again.
  5. Preview the pages, then save to Files or share it from the system share sheet.

What you can do

  • Drop pages the portal does not need before you shrink anything.
  • Check the exact file size against your limit in the result screen.
  • Compare against an original export of the same selected pages.
  • Work on your device. PDFReady has no developer-hosted PDF service or account.

Questions

Will the PDF always fit under my limit?

No. The exported size is measured, never guaranteed. A limit guides compression, and a target can be unattainable. Select fewer pages or choose Smaller if the result is still too big.

Does shrinking change how the PDF looks?

Balanced and Smaller render pages as images to try to reduce size, which lowers image detail and flattens searchable text, links and forms. Check readability in the preview before you upload.

Can it make a small PDF smaller?

Not always. A small vector PDF can actually grow after compression. Original mode keeps text and does not aim to reduce size.

Is there a file size limit for what I import?

Up to 100 MB per imported PDF and 200 pages per workspace. Password-protected PDFs need an unlocked copy first.
