Merge PDFs on iPhone and iPad

Need one file instead of five? PDFReady combines pages from several PDFs into a single new PDF you can arrange, preview and share.

Free to download. Your first 3 PDFs are free. Pro is $4.99 once, or a 7-day free trial then $2.99 per year.

How to do it in PDFReady

  1. Open PDFReady and add each PDF from Files.
  2. Choose the pages you want from each one, individually or as a range such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.
  3. Use Arrange pages to put the packet in the order you want, and give the new PDF a name.
  4. Create the PDF, check the measured size and preview every page.
  5. Save to Files or share it with the system share sheet. Your originals are not changed.

What you can do

  • Combine pages from several PDFs into one new PDF.
  • Add photos and scans to the same packet.
  • Leave out pages you do not need.
  • Reorder pages before you create the file.
  • Check size and preview before sending.

Questions

Do I need an account to merge PDFs?

No. PDFReady has no accounts, ads or analytics, and assembles documents on your device.

Will signatures survive a merge?

Assembling a new PDF can invalidate digital signatures. Keep signed originals and check the result in the recipient’s viewer.

How many pages can I combine?

Up to 200 pages in one workspace, with up to 100 MB per imported PDF.

Does merging shrink the file?

Original mode keeps page text and does not aim to reduce size. Choose Balanced or Smaller if you also need a smaller file.
